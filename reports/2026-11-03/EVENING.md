# Evening Update

## What Changed Today

- **Oil and gas slump on US-Iran talks:** Brent closed near $104.85 a barrel and WTI at $96.36, down about 7.5% (ANSA). Dutch TTF gas fell about 10% to roughly €93/MWh. Reports that Iran's Supreme Leader Khamenei approved talks with Washington drove the move. Trump said a deal "could happen". The war is in its ninth month and Hormuz is still shut.
- **Abu Dhabi talks take shape:** Envoy Steve Witkoff said the US will mediate technical-level Russia-Ukraine talks. He will also meet delegates from France, Germany, the UK, the EU and NATO on security guarantees for Kyiv. The FT calls it the first such US-Russia-Ukraine meeting since February.
- **Russian bombs kill 26 at a Ukrainian aid centre:** Kyiv says at least nine 3,000kg bombs hit a southern village. Zelensky called it "utterly horrific". This is Ukraine's account; Moscow has not confirmed it.
- **US midterms vote today:** The House and a third of the Senate are at stake. Trump closed his campaign at the Lincoln Memorial reflecting pool. An FT poll shows a large majority disapprove of his handling of inflation.
- **AI financing strain deepens:** Oracle is near a $10bn share sale at an 11% discount, per Bloomberg. Anthropic disclosed about $900bn of AI spending commitments (FT) ahead of its Nasdaq listing.
- **China moves to slow the yuan's rally:** The People's Bank of China (PBOC) will raise the foreign-exchange reserve requirement ratio from 15 November. The offshore yuan weakened on the news.
- **US strips South Africa and Rwanda of AGOA access:** Both lose duty-free access under the African Growth and Opportunity Act. This comes a week after US sanctions on Rwanda's defence ministry.
- **Cold-chain hacks reach Italy:** Two Italian food firms were hit. The national cybersecurity agency (ACN) has called in retailers and food-chain operators.

## Europe & Italy

### Italian spread and yields ease with oil
The BTP-Bund spread closed almost 6 points lower at 113 basis points. The 10-year BTP yield fell almost 9 points to 4.42% (ANSA). Cheaper energy helps Italy, a large gas importer, and reduces pressure for more ECB hikes. Milan rose 0.9%, Paris 0.98%, Frankfurt 0.63% and London 0.29% (ANSA).

**Watch:** Eurozone October flash inflation on Wednesday. The September rate was 3.8%.

### Cold-chain cyberattacks reach Italy
A Tuscan catering company and an Emilian frozen-food company were targeted, Il Sole 24 Ore reports. The ACN has stepped up monitoring and convened large retailers and food-chain operators. The attacks follow suspected hacks that pushed freezer temperatures up in six European countries. FT's newsletter says supermarket freezers overheated in a fresh attack. Nobody has been blamed.

**Why it matters:** Attacks on refrigeration control systems can spoil stock and show how weak operational technology (the systems that run physical equipment) can be in food supply.

**Watch:** Any attribution, and any ACN guidance to the sector.

*Sources: [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://www.ilsole24ore.com/art/cyberattacchi-catena-freddo-colpite-due-aziende-italiane-allerta-acn-AJ0l6I2B), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/65982705-e3f3-4dfe-8d5d-39809f8a1103?syn-25a6b1a6=1)*

### Italian car market grows, but Stellantis slips
Registrations rose 4.8% in October and 8.3% over ten months (ANSA). Stellantis, counting Leapmotor, fell 5.5% in the month. Its market share dropped to 24.2%.

**Why it matters:** Italy's largest industrial group is losing share at home even as the market grows.

*Source: [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/economia/2026/11/02/mercato-dellauto-in-crescita-del-48-in-italia-a-ottobre-nei-dieci-mesi-83_126df56f-11d4-454c-99fc-b6f40d624449.html)*

### Lagarde's exit and French industrial stress
Bank of France chief Moulin said ECB President Christine Lagarde resigned to start a new chapter in her career (Bloomberg). Our earlier edition reported she leaves on 7 February 2027. Separately, Alstom shares fell 26% after a second profit warning under new chief Martin Sion. The group also set out a plan to cut €2bn of debt (FT). Alstom has large Italian rail operations, and the fall adds to market worry about French industry.

**Watch:** European leaders' search for Lagarde's successor.

*Sources: [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-02/lagarde-wants-to-kick-off-new-career-phase-ecb-s-moulin-says), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/95723b31-c35b-4d68-9abe-9e082e95e362?syn-25a6b1a6=1)*

### Greece buys KC-390 transports
Greece signed a government-to-government deal with Portugal for three Embraer KC-390 aircraft, with options for up to six more (Breaking Defense). The Brazilian-built jet is a European choice over US rivals.

*Source: [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/11/greece-inks-deal-for-kc-390-fleet/)*

### Russia and Ukraine: diplomacy alongside heavy strikes
Witkoff said talks will be at technical level. The earlier edition put the meeting at 6-10 November. Kyiv says Russian bombs killed 26 people at an aid centre in a southern village. Washington approved a $2.1bn Patriot missile sale to Ukraine, but delivery is not expected until a couple of years into the 2030s (Defense News). Japan Times reports Russia's army is suffering record losses while its land gains slip. The source's analysis was not independently verified here.

Russia's statistics agency Rosstat confirmed a cyberattack claimed by Belarusian and pro-Ukrainian hackers. Fake nuclear warnings went out on Sunday after the air-alert apps in Ukraine and Russia were hacked, and neither government has said who was responsible.

### China: curbing the yuan's rise
The PBOC will raise the FX reserve ratio (the share of foreign currency banks must hold in reserve) from 15 November. That cuts the supply of dollars to markets and slows the yuan's rise. The offshore yuan weakened. Vice Premier He Lifeng pledged support for Hong Kong's open economy and more offshore yuan liquidity. Apple's China sales missed estimates.

## Technology, Defence & Space

### Apple beats on revenue; China is the weak spot
Fiscal fourth-quarter revenue rose 12% to $114.5bn against a $113.5bn consensus (Bloomberg). Growth slowed from 16% in the June quarter. It is the first report since John Ternus became CEO. IDC says the smartphone market fell 21% in the third quarter, while Apple's shipments fell only 4%.

### AI funding: Oracle's discounted raise and Anthropic's IPO
Oracle is near a $10bn share sale at an 11% discount. Anthropic has named eight firms, including Macquarie and CommSec, for an Australian retail offer ahead of its Nasdaq debut. The FT says it has disclosed about $900bn of AI spending commitments. Other reports I found put the figure lower, so treat the total as depending on how commitments are counted.

**Why it matters:** Raising cash on this scale shows how heavily AI build-outs rely on capital markets.
