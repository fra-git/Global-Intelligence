# Europe & Italy

## Italy

Rome's news is dominated by a split inside the League and by the 2027 budget law (*manovra*). Inflation is rising, but markets are calm.

### League moves to oust its Senate leader as the budget reaches parliament
Almost all of the League's 29 senators have signed a petition to remove Massimiliano Romeo as head of the party's Senate group, ANSA reported on 4 November. The exceptions are Romeo himself, Matteo Salvini and a few absentees. Romeo says he will resist. Marco Dreosto and Andrea Paganella are named as possible successors. The Senate has no quorum rule for such a motion, so the party decides. A federal council meets in Milan on Friday.

Romeo backed Massimo Garavaglia against Salvini at the party congress ten days ago, where Garavaglia won 24%. In a linked change, Alberto Gusmeroli replaces Silvana Comaroli, who is seen as close to Economy Minister Giancarlo Giorgetti, as the League's budget rapporteur. The opposition says Salvini is putting Giorgetti under tutelage.

**Why it matters:** Giorgetti is the League's main guarantor of fiscal discipline. Internal pressure on him, especially over pensions, could complicate the budget's passage.

*Sources: [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/politica/2026/11/04/lega-garavaglia-partita-la-raccolta-firme-per-sfiduciare-romeo-e-volonta-di-dividere_50d47089-3fa1-43db-8450-81c4a67f060c.html)*

### Budget: €4.8bn more for defence
Il Sole 24 Ore reports that Defence gets €4.8bn more. Rearmament and security get €10.9bn extra over three years, with 85% concentrated in 2027-28 because of the EU clause that exempts defence spending from the deficit rules. The money covers NATO commitments, the Carabinieri, joint-forces programmes and the Global Combat Air Programme (the Italy-UK-Japan fighter jet).

*Sources: [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://www.ilsole24ore.com/art/investimenti-nato-carabinieri-difesa-48-miliardi-piu-AJJGj94B)*

### Inflation climbs, markets shrug
Eurostat's flash estimate puts euro-area inflation at 4.0% in October, up from 3.8%. ANSA gives Italy at +5.6%, on the EU-harmonised measure. Istat's national index was 5.4% on 30 October, with energy the main driver. Istat also reported that September food sales fell 0.3% month on month.

The BTP-Bund spread, the gap between Italian and German 10-year yields, is about 103-105 basis points, narrower than yesterday's 112. The 10-year BTP yields about 4.30%. Milan's FTSE MIB rose 2.18% on 4 November, led by banks, and was about +0.3% at midday today.

**Business:** BPER's nine-month profit rose 16.4% to €2bn. Brembo's revenue was €2.9bn and its guidance is confirmed. Insolvency liquidations rose 13.6% in the third quarter, according to Il Sole 24 Ore. Meloni welcomed the Electrolux agreement and said the government will monitor compliance.

**Watch:** The League's vote on Romeo, and the 9 November finance ministers' meeting on Italy's request to use the escape clause.

**For Europe & Italy:** Higher rates feed through to mortgages and to the cost of Italian debt. The spread has tightened for now.

## United States

Democrats appear to have won control of both chambers of Congress on Tuesday. These are media projections, not certified results. Trump now faces a hostile House from January, and he is trying to ease fuel prices abroad before then.

### Democrats win Congress; Trump says he accepts the result
NBC projects Democrats will hold at least 259 House seats, their largest majority in almost two decades. Several governorships also flipped. Trump told the New York Times he is "absolutely willing" to accept the outcome and said the vote was not a referendum on him. Vice President JD Vance blamed the losses on the Iran war. ## Russia & Ukraine

### Investing basics: duration
Duration measures how much a bond's price falls when interest rates rise. A bond with duration of 7 years loses roughly 7% if yields rise one percentage point. That is why long bonds suffer when central banks hike, and short bonds are steadier. Duration is the price of earning a higher yield, so match it to how long you can hold the bond.

## Explained

### The EU "escape clause"
It is a provision in the EU fiscal rules that lets a country temporarily exceed its spending limits for specific purposes. Italy asked to use it for defence and energy.

The steps are:
1. The country asks.
2. The Commission assesses it. It gave Italy a go-ahead on 8 October.
3. Finance ministers in the Council approve it.

The Commission's go-ahead came with a one-month window for the Council decision. Think of it as a household allowed to exceed its monthly budget because of a roof repair, as long as the bill is itemised. Today it matters because 85% of Italy's extra €10.9bn for defence and security falls in 2027-28 and depends on it. Key number: €10.9bn over three years.

### Duration and central-bank hikes
Duration estimates how much a bond's price falls when yields rise. A duration of 7 years means roughly a 7% loss if yields rise one percentage point. It works because existing bonds with low coupons become less attractive when new ones pay more, like a fixed-rate loan after rates rise. With the Bank of England hiking and the ECB and Fed possibly following in December, long BTPs and Treasuries are the most exposed. Key number: 4.30% on the 10-year BTP.

### Merger control as a geopolitical lever
Many countries can block a foreign takeover on security grounds. China's foreign-investment security review office reviews deals touching its market, and it can prohibit the China part of a global transaction. A buyer such as SoftBank cannot own a unit without access to the market that makes up most of its value. It is like a landlord with a veto on a shop sale. In the SoftBank-ABB case, the FT cited concerns about data transfers to Japan. Key number: $5.4bn.

### Satellite "rendezvous and proximity operations"
A satellite can manoeuvre close to another to inspect it, or to prepare an attack. In October, Russia's Cosmos 2612 closed from about 350 km to under 5 km of ICEYE-X36, a commercial radar satellite used to support Ukraine. It is like a car tailgating at close range on a motorway: harmless or hostile, it shows capability. Key number: under 5 km.

## Glossary

- **Escape clause:** an EU fiscal-rule exemption allowing temporary extra spending.
- **BTP-Bund spread:** the gap between Italian and German 10-year yields, a gauge of Italian risk. It is about 103-105 basis points.
- **Basis point:** one hundredth of a percentage point.
- **HICP:** the EU-harmonised inflation measure, comparable across countries.
- **Istat:** Italy's national statistics office.
- **PNRR:** Italy's share of the EU recovery fund.
- **Hormuz:** the strait carrying a large share of the world's oil, currently closed.
- **Brent/WTI:** the main European and US oil price benchmarks.
- **TTF:** the Dutch benchmark price for European gas.
- **GCAP:** the Italy-UK-Japan fighter programme.
- **SPAC:** a listed shell company used to take another firm public.
- **Giancarlo Giorgetti:** Italy's Economy Minister, from the League.
