# Evening Update

## What Changed Today

- **Oil's biggest one-day fall in months:** Brent closed at $94.70 (-8.59%) and WTI at $87.93 (-8.49%). Traders bet the Democratic sweep of the US midterms will push Washington toward ending the Iran war. Brent is down about 18% on the week. Dutch TTF gas fell as much as 6.7% to about €85/MWh.
- **Trump ties defeat to Iran war fuel prices:** He acknowledged that higher gasoline prices linked to the war contributed to the Republican loss. Democrats are already promising investigations of the administration and of people and companies close to Trump.
- **Ukraine: new talks, new casualties:** Russian and Ukrainian delegations meet Melania Trump at the White House on Thursday. Zelensky gave her Ukraine's highest award after the Oleshky local ceasefire. Kyiv says at least 40 people died in Monday's strike on a village and about 30 may still be under rubble. It says bodycam video shows a "double-tap" attack, a second strike on rescuers.
- **Eurozone inflation detail:** ANSA reports Italy's October rate at 5.6%, with energy prices up 20.8%, against 4.0% for the euro area. ECB Governing Council member Klaas Knot, a frontrunner to succeed Christine Lagarde, said the bank must stay hawkish.
- **Rheinmetall cuts its outlook:** Germany's largest arms maker no longer expects a big Bundeswehr tank order this year, and a cruise-missile partnership is behind plan. Handelsblatt says the shares reacted sharply.
- **Italy's Lega splits:** Signatures are being collected to oust Massimiliano Romeo as Senate group leader. The budget rapporteur changed to Gusmeroli, and pressure on Economy Minister Giorgetti is growing.
- **European Parliament softens 2035 car rule:** Il Sole 24 Ore reports a first parliamentary vote backing a lower fleet-wide CO2 cut for 2035, 87.5% instead of 100%.
- **Cyber:** A French bank, Crédit Mutuel Arkéa, confirmed a heist of an estimated $133m through a compromised supplier. A video claiming the freezer attacks has not settled the case.

## Europe & Italy

### Lega turns on its Senate leader as the budget moves
Signatures are circulating in the Lega to remove Massimiliano Romeo as Senate group leader, ANSA reports. Romeo says he will resist. The budget bill's rapporteur has changed too: Mario Gusmeroli takes over, and pressure on Giorgetti is rising.

**Why it matters:** The Lega is a core government party and Giorgetti runs the budget. An internal fight during the budget session can slow amendments and weaken the coalition's message.

**Watch:** Whether the signature drive reaches a formal vote in the Senate group.

*Sources: [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/politica/2026/11/04/lega-garavaglia-partita-la-raccolta-firme-per-sfiduciare-romeo-e-volonta-di-dividere_50d47089-3fa1-43db-8450-81c4a67f060c.html)*

### Italian inflation and a tighter BTP spread
ANSA reports that Italian inflation reached 5.6% in October, with energy up 20.8%. The euro-area rate is 4.0%, up from 3.8%. The BTP-Bund spread, the gap between Italian and German 10-year yields, narrowed to 104.2 basis points. The BTP yields 4.3% and the Bund 3.2%.

**Why it matters:** Energy drives the rise, so cheaper oil and gas would ease it. Klaas Knot told Bloomberg the ECB will likely have to stay hawkish and watch for inflation shocks.

**Market impact:** A tighter spread helps Italy's debt costs and banks. The FTSE MIB ended flat at 49,942 (-0.01%), while the Euro Stoxx 50 lost 0.51%.

**Watch:** Rome's request to use the EU escape clause for defence and energy goes to finance ministers around 9 November.

*Sources: [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/economia/2026/11/04/lo-spread-btp-bund-si-stringe-a-1042-punti_267446e3-23a5-4453-baa9-21654148678c.html), [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/economia/2026/11/04/linflazione-e-salita-al-4-nelleurozona-a-ottobre_322a1cda-fea3-4327-b408-42a920c45bfc.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-04/knot-says-ecb-will-need-to-stick-with-a-hawkish-stance-on-policy)*

### Electrolux deal and Rheinmetall's warning
Meloni called the Electrolux agreement an important step for protecting Italian industry. The government will monitor compliance. In Germany, Rheinmetall said it no longer expects a large tank order from the Bundeswehr this year.

**For Europe & Italy:** Slow procurement hits European defence budgets and suppliers. Italy's Leonardo is a partner of Rheinmetall in a tank programme, though today's report does not mention it.

*Sources: [ANSA](https://www.ansa.it/sito/notizie/politica/2026/11/04/meloni-intesa-electrolux-passo-importante-per-la-tutela-dellindustria-italiana_1e97e64e-9bfe-4866-8cc8-85a7c3d65b9a.html), [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/unternehmen/industrie/ruestungsindustrie-verzoegerungen-bei-auftraegen-rheinmetall-senkt-prognose/100267059.html)*

### EU car rules and Ukraine's accession
A first parliamentary vote backs cutting the 2035 car-fleet CO2 target from 100% to 87.5%. Other reporting says a plenary vote could come in December, and the Council votes afterwards. Separately, the FT says Brussels suggests different dates for finishing membership talks with Ukraine and Moldova, which applied together. Ukraine now trails Moldova.

**Why it matters:** The car vote is a major concession to carmakers. The accession split shows the EU's caution on Ukraine.

*Sources: [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://www.ilsole24ore.com/art/auto-primo-si-parlamento-ue-flessibilita-target-taglio-emissioni-2035-AJwKji4B), [FT](https://www.ft.com/content/6ccf470f-176f-436f-baa7-e5ac6b4cf20f?syn-25a6b1a6=1)*

### Freezer attacks and a French bank heist
NoName and Z-Peintest claimed the freezer attacks in a video that arrived more than three weeks after the first incidents. Il Sole 24 Ore says authorities are not ruling out a decoy or a state actor behind the French cases. Italy's national cybersecurity agency (ACN) renewed its call for urgent checks of refrigeration systems. Separately, Crédit Mutuel Arkéa confirmed attackers made transfers from the bank's own account after compromising a supplier, an estimated $133m.

*Sources: [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://www.ilsole24ore.com/art/cyberattacchi-frigo-noname-e-z-peintest-rivendicano-ma-video-non-chiude-caso-francia-possibile-un-altra-matrice-statuale-AJDjeg4B), [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://www.ilsole24ore.com/art/catena-freddo-nuova-allerta-acn-verifiche-urgenti-sistemi-refrigerazione-AJ5Qsy4B), [The Record](https://therecord.media/french-bank-confirms-cyber-heist)*

## World
